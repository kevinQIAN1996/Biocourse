  2. [2]

    Andreae, W. A. & Good, N. E. (1955). The formation of indoleacetylaspartic acid in pea seedlings. Plant Physiology 30, 380–382. DOI 10.1104/pp.30.4.380.

    原论文涉及IAA缀合，不能作为“一种氧化酶破坏所有天然激素而不作用任何合成物”的证明。后续氧化证据：Zhang, J. et al. (2016). DAO1 catalyzes temporal and tissue-specific oxidative inactivation of auxin in Arabidopsis thaliana. PNAS 113, 11010–11015. DOI 10.1073/pnas.1604769113。

模型、引用与课堂用语边界
